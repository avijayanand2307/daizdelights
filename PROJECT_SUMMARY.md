## 🎨 Customization Examples

### Change Primary Color
Edit `styles.css` line 8:
```css
--primary-color: #8B4513;  /* Change to your color */
```

### Update Product
Edit `index.html` product section:
```html
<div class="product-card">
    <h3>Your Product Name</h3>
    <span class="price">$XX.XX</span>
</div>
```

### Change Contact Info
Edit `index.html` contact section:
```html
<p>(555) 123-4567</p>  <!-- Change to your phone -->
<p>hello@daizdelights.com</p>  <!-- Change to your email -->
```
